| T-10-01 | Spoofing | `doPost` getAdminData | mitigate | Passcode validated via `PropertiesService.getScriptProperties()` BEFORE any sheet access. Invalid passcode → immediate `{ ok: false, error: "Invalid passcode" }` response with no data. |
